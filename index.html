Questions

Everything you're wondering.

Do I need to be good with technology?
Not at all. If you can send a text message, you can run Orchamind. You can type or simply talk to it — say "log 8 hours for Mike at the Johnson job" and it handles the rest. It was built specifically for trades people, not tech people.
What kinds of businesses use it?
Any business that runs jobs with a field team: general contractors, electricians, plumbers, HVAC, roofers, painters, landscapers, pool builders, cleaning and janitorial, pest control, solar, property maintenance, moving companies, and more. It's set up with your industry's language.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes. Each company gets its own private, secure system with individual logins. Owners and managers see financials and budgets; crew members only see the jobs they're assigned to. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text.
Can my crew see my budgets and margins?
Only if you let them. Access is role-based: you decide who sees money and who doesn't. Crew members see their assigned jobs and log their hours — never your financials.
Does it know building codes and permits?
Yes. The AI knows general California building-code and permitting basics — when permits are typically needed, the standard inspection sequence, CALGreen and Title 24 requirements, and Southern California specifics for Los Angeles, Orange County, and the wider region. It always reminds you to confirm exact requirements with your local building department, since codes are local and change. It helps you avoid the costly mistake of covering up work before an inspection.
Can it do payroll and reports?
Yes. With one click you get total hours per worker for the pay period — ready for payroll — plus job-cost reports showing budget versus spent on every job. Everything exports to Excel or PDF, so you can hand it straight to your bookkeeper. You can filter by this week, a pay period, or custom dates.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — phone, tablet, and desktop. Most owners run it from their phone in the field, and the whole team stays in sync on the same live information.
