How the estimator works

Start the estimate — describe the job in plain words, or tap “From plans” and add photos or a PDF.
AI drafts and previews — itemized lines appear with a headline total, $/sq ft, and a 3D model when plans are attached.
Answer the ask-backs — fixture grade, ceiling height, demo included? A few taps and the numbers refine.
Review and price — confirm quantities, apply your rates and markup. You always have the last word.
Send and convert — deliver a branded estimate, then turn the accepted one into an invoice in a click.
