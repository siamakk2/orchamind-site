# Exquisite Fabric — Store Profile

Every job reads this file. Update it when the owner corrects the agent; corrections are rules.

## Facts
- Store: Exquisite Fabric — exquisitefabric.com (Shopify)
- Customer inbox: admin@exquisitefabric.com
- Suppliers: many (list to be built from Shopify `vendor` field and supplier email history)
- Currency: USD (confirm from get-shop-info)

## Hard limits — never without the owner's explicit yes
- Send any email, message, or review request to a customer or supplier
- Place, send, or change a purchase order
- Issue a refund, discount, or store credit
- Publish, unpublish, re-price, or delete a product
- Change the theme, site code, robots.txt, or llms.txt
- Promise a delivery or restock date

## Working rules (defaults until the owner says otherwise)
- Replies: warm, knowledgeable about fabric, short. Sign as "Exquisite Fabric".
- Swatch requests: always answered same day in the draft queue.
- Wholesale / trade / bulk-yardage inquiries: flag to the owner, never auto-draft pricing.
- Complaints, damaged goods, chargebacks: top of NEEDS YOU, with the order pulled up.
- Restock: flag any fabric projected to run out within its supplier lead time + 14 days.
- Low-value noise (orders under USD 10, spam) is summarized, not itemized.
