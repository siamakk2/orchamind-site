How it works

Talk to it. It does the rest.

No training, no manuals, no clunky software. Orchamind works the way you already think and talk about your jobs, from the first set of plans to the final invoice.

Start free — first month on usTry the live demo
Four steps

From day one to running every job by voice.

1

Add your jobs and crew

Type them in or just say them out loud. Your whole operation lives in one place, synced across your team.

2

Run your day by voice

“Log 8 hours for Mike at the Johnson job.” “Move Carlos to the Napa site.” It understands and does it.

3

Get your morning briefing

Each day the AI reads your whole operation and tells you what needs attention first, before you’ve had coffee.

4

Catch problems early

Risk alerts flag tight budgets, slipping jobs and upcoming inspections while there’s still time to act.

Just talk to it
