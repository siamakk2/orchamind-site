Orchamind is pleased to announce that Matthew Ministri has joined the company as Head of Sales, taking ownership of the front office as Orchamind moves from early trials into full-scale adoption among contractors nationwide.

Matthew is passionate about helping businesses grow by combining innovative technology with practical, customer-focused solutions. Throughout his career, he has built a reputation for developing strong relationships, understanding complex business challenges, and delivering meaningful results through a consultative approach.

With years of experience working alongside business owners across a variety of industries, Matthew has gained valuable insight into the challenges of running and scaling a successful company — helping organizations of all sizes improve efficiency, streamline operations, and achieve sustainable growth.

"Technology should make running a business easier, not more complicated. I'm committed to building intelligent, intuitive solutions that give contractors back their time — for their customers, their crews, and their growth."

— Matthew Ministri

Matthew believes the best products are built by listening to customers and solving real-world problems — the same mindset that grounds Orchamind's mission to help contractors embrace the future of AI-powered business management.

Who is Matthew Ministri?
