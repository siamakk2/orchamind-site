Software for general contractors

A general contractor juggles estimates, crews, subs, budgets and clients — often from a truck. The right software pulls it into one place and does the busywork. Here's what to look for.

The short answer

The best software for a general contractor keeps estimating, job tracking, crew and hours, subcontractors and invoicing in one place — and increasingly uses AI to draft estimates, flag at-risk jobs and answer questions about your own projects. Orchamind was built for exactly this: one workspace with an AI that understands your whole operation.

What a general contractor actually needs

Estimating and bids that don't underprice the work.
Job and project tracking with budget versus spent.
Crew scheduling and hours.
Subcontractor tracking.
Invoicing and a clear way to get paid.
Works on a phone — because you're rarely at a desk.

Why AI changes the game for GCs

Older contractor software just stores data. The newer generation acts on it. Orchamind gives you a morning briefing of what needs attention, a risk radar that flags jobs drifting over budget or behind schedule, an AI that drafts estimates and concept drawings, and an assistant that actually knows your jobs, crew and numbers. Less like a filing cabinet, more like a foreman who never sleeps.

One place instead of five apps

Most GCs stitch together a spreadsheet, a notes app, a separate invoicing tool and their memory — and that's where things fall through. Orchamind keeps projects, crew, hours, subs, estimates, invoices and concept plans in a single workspace, so nothing gets lost between apps.
