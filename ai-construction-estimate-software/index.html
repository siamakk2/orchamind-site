AI Estimating

Can AI write a construction estimate?

Short answer: yes. Describe the job in plain English and modern AI can draft an itemized estimate — line items, quantities and pricing — in seconds. Here's how it works, and where a human still matters.

The short answer

AI can write a construction estimate by turning a plain-language scope ("rewire a 2,000 sq ft home, 200A panel, 24 recessed lights") into itemized line items with quantities and realistic unit pricing. You review and adjust the numbers, then send it as a professional estimate or invoice. It removes the blank page and the math — you keep final say on price.

What AI estimating actually does

An AI estimator reads your description of the job and produces a structured list: each task or material as a line item, with a quantity, a unit and a unit price. Instead of staring at a blank spreadsheet, you start from a complete first draft and refine it. In Orchamind you type the scope, tap Generate, and the estimate builds itself — then you tweak any number before it goes out.

Why it helps contractors win and protect margin

Two things quietly kill contractor profit: underbidding and slow quoting. AI attacks both. It won't forget a line the way a tired human at 9pm does, and it turns a two-hour estimate into a two-minute one — so you quote while the lead is still hot. Faster, more complete estimates mean more jobs won at prices that actually pay you.

Does AI replace your judgment?

No — and it shouldn't. AI gives you a fast, complete starting point using typical regional pricing. You know your crew's real production rates, your supplier costs this week and the quirks of the site. The workflow that works: let AI draft, you adjust, you approve. The estimate is yours; the AI just did the typing and the math.
