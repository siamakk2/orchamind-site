Pricing questions

Straight answers.

How much does Orchamind cost?

The platform is $500 per month per company, and your first month is free. There is no per-seat charge for your crew.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You can start your free month without a card and decide afterwards.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term contract; cancel whenever you like.

What is the $750 website?

A one-time, done-for-you professional website for your contracting business: copy, photos, mobile-fast, Google-ready, with a lead form and click-to-call. It is separate from the platform subscription.

Can I get both?

Yes. Most contractors do both. We build the site and set up the platform together; just ask.

How does it compare on price to ServiceTitan or Buildertrend?

Those tools are priced for larger companies and often charge per user. Orchamind is one flat company price built for small and mid-size contractors. See the side-by-side comparison.

Your first month is on us.
