AI Drawings

Can AI draw floor plans and electrical or plumbing layouts?

Yes — AI can generate concept drawings from a plain description in seconds. They're perfect for showing a client the idea and giving your engineer a head start. They are not a replacement for stamped, permit-ready plans. Here's the honest picture.

The short answer

AI can draw concept floor plans, site plans and lighting, outlet, plumbing, HVAC, framing, roofing and solar layouts from a plain-English description in seconds. These are concept drawings — great for winning the job and briefing your engineer — but they are not to-scale, engineered or permit-ready. For stamped structural, electrical or plumbing plans you still need a licensed engineer or architect.

What AI can draw today

Describe the space and AI can produce a labeled concept diagram: a floor plan with rooms and rough sizes, a site plan with setbacks, a lighting-and-outlet layout, a one-line electrical diagram, a plumbing fixture layout, an HVAC duct concept, a framing idea, a roofing plan or a solar panel layout. In Orchamind you pick the type, describe the job and it draws it right on screen — then you print it or attach it to your estimate.

Where AI drawings shine

They close the gap between "let me get back to you" and winning the job. Showing a homeowner a concept in the first meeting makes you look organized and lets them picture the result. And when it's time for real plans, handing your engineer a clear concept cuts the back-and-forth — which saves you time and money.

When you still need a licensed engineer or architect

This is the honest part. Structural, electrical and plumbing plans submitted for a building permit must be prepared and stamped by a licensed engineer or architect. A building department will reject unstamped plans, and building off an unengineered drawing is a safety and liability risk. That's exactly why AI concept drawings are labeled “concept only — not for construction or permit.” Think of AI as the tool that gets you to your engineer faster and cheaper — not one that replaces them.
