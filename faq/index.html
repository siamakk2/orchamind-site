Orchamind: frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about Orchamind — what it is, how the AI works, how it compares, pricing, security, and getting started.

Orchamind is an AI-native operating system for contractors and field-service businesses. It handles leads, estimates, invoices, scheduling, jobs, customers, crew, and reporting — and adds AI that writes your estimates, follows up on leads, flags risky jobs, and answers questions about your business. You can even run it by voice.

Orchamind is built for contractors and trades — general contractors, electricians, plumbers, HVAC, remodelers, landscapers, and other field-service businesses — especially small-to-midsize shops that want enterprise-level intelligence without enterprise cost or complexity.

Orchamind matches Jobber and Housecall Pro on the core workflows — scheduling, estimates, invoicing, client portal, reminders, and review requests — but it is built AI-first. It writes estimates and proposals, drafts lead follow-ups, warns you when a job is trending over budget, and can be operated entirely by voice. Those AI and voice capabilities are what set it apart.

Orchamind’s AI drafts estimates and proposals from a short description, writes follow-up messages to new leads, flags jobs heading over budget through its risk radar, generates plans and sketches, and acts as a business adviser you can ask about your numbers — like “which customers haven’t paid?” or “what’s my profit this month?”
