The short version

A project manager who never sleeps — that you run by talking.

Most field businesses run on memory, sticky notes, and texts that get lost. Hours go untracked, materials get forgotten, and at the end of the month nobody really knows which jobs made money. Orchamind fixes that without making you learn software.

If you can send a text message, you can run Orchamind. You talk to it the way you'd talk to your sharpest foreman — "Log 8 hours for Mike at the Johnson job, we used 40 sheets of drywall from Home Depot" — and it files everything in the right place. No forms. No training. No typing if you don't want to.

How it works

Three steps. That's the whole system.

No setup headaches. You speak, the AI organizes, and you get a clear picture of your business — updated in real time.

1

You talk (or tap)

Tell Orchamind what happened on the job — hours, crew, materials, progress, problems. Speak it in one breath or tap a few buttons. Whatever's easier in the field.

2

The AI organizes it

It figures out which job, which crew member, which cost — and files it instantly. It catches duplicates, flags price jumps, and keeps your numbers straight.

3

You see everything

Jobs, hours, costs, crew, and risks — all in one clean dashboard, plus a daily AI briefing that tells you what needs attention before it becomes a problem.

Capabilities

Every function, explained.

Here's everything Orchamind can do for your business today — each one built to save you time, money, or a headache.

Zero-type logging

Run your whole day by voice

"Log 8 hours for Mike at the Johnson job."

Log hours, assign crew, add jobs, set progress, note problems — just by talking. No menus to hunt through, no forms to fill. Perfect for muddy hands and bright sunlight where typing is a pain.

Why it matters: The #1 reason hours and costs go untracked is friction. Remove the friction and nothing slips through — which means accurate payroll and job costing without the office work.
Materials & cost tracking

Every material logged to the right job

"We used 40 sheets of drywall from Home Depot, $480."

Say what you bought and Orchamind adds it to that job's costs and notes automatically. It even flags when a price jumps unusually from last time, so you catch overcharges and waste.

Why it matters: Materials are where job profit quietly leaks. Tracking them in real time tells you which jobs are actually making money — while you can still do something about it.
Daily reports

One paragraph in, a full day logged

"Today on the Smith job: crew of 3, 24 hours total, poured the foundation, ordered rebar, slight delay on inspection."

Speak your end-of-day rundown in plain language and Orchamind breaks it into hours, crew, progress, materials, and notes — all filed at once, then read back to you so you can confirm.

Why it matters: A complete daily record with zero paperwork. Great for disputes, change orders, and knowing exactly what happened on any day months later.
Offline-first

No signal? Keep working anyway

Basements, new builds, rural sites — Orchamind saves everything right on your phone the instant you log it, then syncs to the cloud automatically the moment you're back online. A little status pill always tells you whether you're synced or saved on device.

Why it matters: Field work happens in dead zones. Software that needs a perfect connection gets abandoned. This one just works, then catches up.
AI assistant & daily briefing

Ask anything. Get a morning game plan.

"Which jobs are over budget?" · "How many hours did we put on the Smith job?"

Ask Orchamind questions about your own business in plain English and get straight answers. Every morning it gives you a briefing — what needs attention, what's at risk, what's falling behind — so you start the day ahead of problems instead of behind them.

Why it matters: The information was always buried in your jobs. Now it talks back — like having an analyst who's read every record and tells you what matters.
Reports & 1-click export

Your data is yours — download it anytime

Clean reports across jobs, hours, crew, and subcontractors, plus a one-click export of everything to a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full backup file (JSON). No lock-in, no "export fees," no calling support.

Why it matters: Send hours to your bookkeeper, hand costs to your accountant, or keep your own backup — in seconds. You own your numbers.
QuickBooks sync

Your books fill themselves in

Connect QuickBooks once and your logged job costs and materials flow straight into your accounting as bills — with duplicate-proof locks so nothing ever posts twice. Start with a "Send to QuickBooks" button you control, then flip it to fully automatic when you're ready.

Why it matters: The bridge between the field and the books is where hours of data entry — and costly mistakes — usually live. This closes it.
Growth tools

Free site audit & instant proposals

Beyond running the back office, Orchamind helps you win work: a free audit that scores your website and shows exactly what to fix to get found on Google, plus a proposal generator that turns job details into a polished client proposal in seconds.

Why it matters: More found jobs, faster bids, and a more professional look — the things that actually grow a field business.
Who sees what

Everyone gets their own view — and only their view.

Orchamind has role-based logins, so your crew sees their jobs while your money stays private. Each company is its own secure, separate system.

Owner

The full picture.

  • All jobs, costs & profit
  • Financials & reports
  • Add & manage the whole team
  • Reset any member's password
  • QuickBooks & exports

Manager

Runs the day-to-day.

  • Assign crew & jobs
  • Log hours & materials
  • Track progress & risks
  • Daily reports & briefings

Crew

Just their work.

  • See only assigned jobs
  • Log their own hours
  • Note materials & progress
  • No access to financials

Private & secure by design. Every company runs in its own isolated system. Passwords are encrypted (never stored as plain text), and owners can reset a team member's password in one tap if someone gets locked out.
