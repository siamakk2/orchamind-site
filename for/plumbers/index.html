For this trade specifically

Built around how plumbing work actually runs.

Plumbing is the trade where the schedule is a suggestion. A water heater fails, a line bursts, and the day you planned is gone. Orchamind is built for that reality: log the emergency by voice from the truck, and the AI tells you which scheduled job slips and who to move. On the project side, it tracks rough-in and final inspections so your work never gets covered before it's signed off, and it keeps material costs — fixtures, copper, PEX, water heaters — tied to each job so you see margin in real time instead of at tax time. Homeowners get automatic progress updates, which cuts the "any news?" calls that interrupt your actual work.
