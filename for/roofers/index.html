Built around how roofing work actually runs.

Roofing has two enemies: weather and documentation. A rain week moves every crew and every promised date, and every insurance job lives or dies on whether you photographed the damage properly. Orchamind takes both. When the forecast turns, reschedule the whole dispatch board in one pass and let AI send every affected homeowner a warm, professional heads-up — instead of you making twelve apology calls. On the documentation side, every job carries a timestamped photo log built as your crew works, so when an adjuster asks for proof of the decking condition, it's already there.
