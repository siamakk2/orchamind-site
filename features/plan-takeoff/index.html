Plan takeoff FAQs

What file types can I upload?

PDF, PNG, and JPG. Upload a plan set or snap photos of the key sheets with your phone.

How does the AI get the square footage right?

It reads the printed area schedule and treats those numbers as ground truth rather than guessing from the drawing, then reconciles the geometry to the stated area — so a plan that prints 2,054 SF reads 2,054 SF.

Does it show a 3D model?

Yes — uploaded plans generate a 3D preview that starts as a glowing blueprint hologram and resolves into a solid model with the correct roof type and story count.

Is the takeoff accurate enough to bid?

It's a strong first draft, not a final bid. Every assumption is listed, unspecified details come back as questions, and you confirm quantities and apply markup before sending.
