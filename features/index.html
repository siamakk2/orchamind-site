Estimating & Takeoff
Turn a plan — or a plain sentence — into a priced estimate

Orchamind's AI estimator does the two things that take contractors the most time: reading plans and pricing the work. Describe a job in a sentence, or upload the actual plans, and get an itemized estimate back in seconds.

AI estimating from a sentence

Type or say "Rewire a 2,000 sq ft home, 200A panel, 24 recessed lights" and get a full itemized estimate with California-realistic pricing you can edit line by line.

Plan takeoff from PDF, PNG & JPG

Upload construction plans in any common format. Orchamind reads the printed area schedule as the source of truth for square footage, uses the marked dimensions, and drafts the takeoff.

3D plan-to-building preview NEW

Watch the plan materialize as a glowing blueprint hologram, then resolve into a solid 3D model — flat, low-slope, gable, or hip roof — matched to the real building. Plus a photoreal render option.

Good / Better / Best proposals

Field & Crew
The crew takes photos. Orchamind writes the report.

No forms, no typing. Your crew shoots the work on site and Orchamind reads every photo against the criteria for that trade — then writes a progress report for the office and a friendly update for the homeowner.

AI Job Report from photos NEW

Upload as many job-site photos as you need. AI estimates percent complete, lists what's done and in progress, flags quality and safety issues, and drafts the next steps.

Trade-specific inspection criteria NEW

Framing checks stud spacing and hangers. Roofing checks flashing and ponding. Fourteen trades, each with its own checklist, so the report judges the work the way a superintendent would.

GPS + timestamped, tamper-evident photos NEW

Every photo is stamped with date, time, job, company, and GPS coordinates, then SHA-256 hashed so it's tamper-evident — the kind of dated proof insurers and disputes require.

Voice job management

Track jobs, crews, and hours just by talking. If a crew member can send a text, they can run Orchamind from the field.

Frequently asked questions

What does Orchamind do for contractors?

It runs a contracting business end to end: writes estimates from a plain-English description, reads uploaded plans and performs an itemized takeoff, shows a 3D preview of the building, turns job-site photos into progress reports, tracks jobs and crews by voice, and handles the client portal, scheduling, and billing — one A-to-Z system instead of a stack of separate tools.

How does Orchamind estimate from construction plans?

Upload a plan as a PDF, PNG, or JPG. Orchamind finds the printed area schedule and treats it as ground truth for square footage, uses the marked dimensions, identifies the roof type and number of stories, and drafts an itemized estimate you review line by line — plus a 3D preview of the property.

What is the AI Job Report feature?

Crews take as many photos as needed on site. Orchamind reads every photo against trade-specific inspection criteria for 14 trades, estimates percent complete, flags quality and safety issues, and writes a homeowner-friendly update. Each photo is stamped with date, time, job, company, and GPS, and SHA-256 hashed so it's tamper-evident for insurance and disputes.

Does Orchamind replace multiple contractor tools?

Yes. Most contractors juggle separate tools for estimating, takeoff, project photos, scheduling, client communication, and invoicing. Orchamind brings all of it into one AI-driven platform — everything the individual tools do, combined A to Z.
