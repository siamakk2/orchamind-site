Material calculator FAQs

How does Orchamind calculate fence materials?

You describe the fence in plain words. Orchamind identifies the type (wire/field/vineyard, wood/board, or chain-link), then calculates T-posts, wood corner and brace posts, wire or rails or boards, gates, concrete, and hardware with proper spacing — a complete bill of materials you can add to your estimate.

How many countertop slabs will I need?

Give your slab size and the size of every countertop piece. Orchamind runs a real 2D nesting layout — placing each piece onto slabs with rotation and blade kerf — and tells you exactly how many slabs the job needs.

Does it show leftover remnants?

Yes. It lists the usable leftover remnant rectangles with real dimensions and square footage, so you can see whether a piece is big enough to reuse on another job.

Is the slab layout a fabricator cut sheet?

It's a rectangular-nesting estimate that includes blade kerf — accurate for planning slab counts and remnants, but not a vein-matched CNC cut sheet. Verify with your fabricator before ordering stone.
